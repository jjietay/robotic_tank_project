My Journey
==================

This section captures the learning journey behind the autonomous tank project:
what you tried, what broke, how you fixed it, and what you learned along the way.

The journey is split into two parts:

- **Reflections** – chronological entries with difficulties faced, solutions,
  photos, and videos.
- **ROS 2 Notes** – structured notes on ROS 2 concepts, interfaces, and patterns
  that you’re learning and applying on the tank.
