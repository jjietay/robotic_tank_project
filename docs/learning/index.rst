My Journey
==================

This section captures my learning journey behind the autonomous tank project:
what I've tried, what broke, how I fixed it, and what I've learned along the way, all written by me.

The journey is split into two parts:

- **Reflections** - chronological entries with difficulties faced, solutions,
  photos, and videos.
- **ROS 2 Notes** - structured notes on ROS 2 concepts, interfaces, and patterns
  that I've learnt and applied on the tank.
